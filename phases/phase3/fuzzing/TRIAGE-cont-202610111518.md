## Generator issues (leads)

### GEN-001: float32 constant overflow (seed 2002664)
`cannot convert (constant 1.56977e+39 of type float64) to type float32`.
The generator emits `float32(<large constant>)` where the constant overflows
float32. Needs a range check on float32 conversions of constants.

### GEN-002/003: exponential string growth (seeds 2002511, 2002138)
Pattern: `v11 = ("..." + (... (v11) ... (v11) ...))` inside a loop.
The string doubles each iteration → 2.3GB and 5.3GB outputs.
The loop-nesting cap (depth 2) does not prevent this; need to avoid
self-referential string assignments in loops, or cap string growth.

### GC_HANG (seeds 2002616, +1)
Under investigation — may be slow programs (not infinite loops) given the
10s gc timeout. The loop cap ensures termination, but 20×20 iterations of
expensive operations can exceed 10s.
